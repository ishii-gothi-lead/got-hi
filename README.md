## 📌 Mission Statement

As synthetic content saturates search results, social feeds, and online communities, distinguishing authentic human interaction from automated noise has become one of the internet's greatest challenges.

**Got HI** is an open-source, user-controlled browser extension engineered to give individuals sovereign control over their browsing experience. Rather than relying on centralized black-box algorithms or opaque platforms, **Got HI** empowers users with configurable filtering layers to surface high-signal, human-authored content.

---

## ⚙️ Core Governance Principles

To remain true to its purpose as a Digital Public Good, **Got HI** operates under six inviolable governance pillars:

1. **User Sovereignty First:** Every filter, rule, and setting remains strictly under the individual user's control. No global censorship or forced defaults.
2. **Open Source & Auditable:** All code, heuristics, and community-maintained lists are published under the **MIT License** for full public scrutiny.
3. **Privacy by Design:** Zero tracking, zero telemetry on private browsing habits, and zero collection of personal user data.
4. **Proactive, Not Reactive:** Focused on highlighting authentic human signal rather than waging an endless, punitive war against synthetic generation.
5. **Non-Automated Authority:** Algorithmic systems shall never hold final authority over content flagging or community guidelines; human review and consensus remain paramount.
6. **Public Review & Transparency:** Major architecture decisions and rule updates must pass through transparent public discussion on GitHub.

## 🚀 Getting Started (Contributors & Developers)

We are actively seeking open-source developers, UI/UX designers, extension engineers, and community organizers to help build the foundational MVP!

### Tech Stack Targets
* **Frontend / UI:** WebExtensions API (Manifest V3 - Chrome, Firefox, Edge, Safari)
* **Core Engine:** TypeScript / JavaScript (Node.js ecosystem)
* **Rule Syntax:** Declarative content filtering & community-maintained list parsers

### How to Contribute
1. **Explore the Issues:** Check out open issues marked `good first issue` or `help wanted`.
2. **Propose Architecture Ideas:** Start a thread in [Discussions](https://github.com/ishii-gothi-lead/got-hi/discussions).
3. **Submit a Pull Request:** Fork the repository, create a branch, and submit your PR for public review!
